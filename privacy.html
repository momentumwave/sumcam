Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data Collection

SumCam processes all data locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. The camera feed and any numbers you scan never leave your device.

Camera Usage

SumCam requires camera access to detect and recognize numbers. The camera feed is processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework and is not recorded or transmitted anywhere.

Local Storage

Any data you choose to save (such as session history) is stored locally on your device using iOS's standard data storage mechanisms. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Third Parties

SumCam does not share any data with third parties. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

SumCam is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
